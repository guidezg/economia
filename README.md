# 💶 €conomia – The Monetary Policy Game (ECB Replica)

A browser-based replica of **€conomia**, the classic European Central Bank (ECB) educational monetary policy simulation. Step into the role of President of the Governing Council for an 8-year term (32 quarters) and steer the Euro Area economy toward price stability.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Your primary mandate is to keep annual inflation **below, but close to, 2.00%** over your 8-year presidency (2011–2018).

* **Set the Key Interest Rate:** Adjust the rate between `0.00%` and `20.00%` in `0.25%` increments each quarter.
* **Manage Time Lags:** Rate changes take 4 to 8 quarters to fully transmit to consumer prices and production.
* **Earn Gold Stars ⭐:** At the end of each year (every 4 quarters), your average inflation and inflation volatility are evaluated. Keep inflation near `1.90%` with low variation to earn up to 8 Gold Stars and secure a top spot in the **Hall of Fame** 🏆.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **📊 Two-Pillar Macroeconomic Engine:** Simulates GDP production growth, HICP inflation, M3 broad money growth, and unemployment with realistic transmission lags and dynamic 3-quarter forecast fan charts.
* **🗺️ 3D Isometric Euro Area Map:** Visualizes the Eurozone with animated wind turbines, rivers, and dynamic weather/market themes that shift when economic shocks hit.
* **⚡ Exogenous Economic Shocks:** Navigate unexpected events—including stock market crashes, rallies, severe floods, winter freezes, oil price hikes, export booms, and confidence slumps—plus manual shock trigger buttons for testing.
* **👥 Governing Council Advisory Team:** Consult 5 distinct advisors around the council table (Growth, Inflation, Monetary Pillar, Employment, and Chief Stability) for real-time metrics and tactical recommendations.
* **📰 Dynamic Press Room:** Read 3 interactive newspapers (*Financial Gazette*, *The European Herald*, and *Frankfurt Chronicle*) with headlines that react to your rate decisions and current economic conditions.
* **📖 Built-in Monetary Policy Glossary:** Searchable encyclopedia explaining key central banking concepts like M3 money growth, transmission lags, and price stability.
* **🔊 Native Web Audio Synthesizer:** Zero-dependency sound effects for slider ticks, tab switches, shock alerts, annual reviews, and trophy fanfares.
* **💾 Local Hall of Fame:** Saves high scores and star counts to `localStorage` alongside historical central bank leaders.

## 🚀 Getting Started

This project is built as a **single, self-contained HTML file** with zero external dependencies, build steps, or assets required. Available through GitHub Pages.

---

## 🕹️ Controls & Navigation

* **Bottom Rate Slider:** Drag the callout box or click `<` / `>` to adjust the Key Interest Rate by `0.25%`, then click **OK** to advance to the next quarter.
* **Hotbar Tabs:**
* **Indicators:** View historical M3 money growth and 3-quarter forecast uncertainty ranges for inflation and production.
* **Your Team:** Click any advisor at the council table for tailored policy advice.
* **Map:** Monitor the main Euro Area multi-line chart, 5 color-coded metric pills, and the isometric map.
* **Press Room:** Click any newspaper front page to zoom in and read the full article.
* **Glossary:** Search and browse core ECB monetary policy terms.
